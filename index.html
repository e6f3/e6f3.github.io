Study Skills

Learn how to learn.

These research-backed strategies are taught and practiced in every session.

🔁

Spaced Repetition

Reviewing material over several days beats one long cram session. Try reviewing after 1 day, 3 days, and 1 week to move knowledge into long-term memory.

🧠

Active Recall

Close the book and test yourself. Retrieving information from memory strengthens it far more than re-reading does.

🗣️

The Feynman Technique

Explain a topic in simple words as if teaching a younger student. Any place you get stuck shows exactly what to study next.

📝

Cornell Notes

Divide the page into cues, notes, and a summary. Writing questions in the margin turns notes into a ready-made study guide.

⏱️

Pomodoro Focus

Work for 25 focused minutes, then rest for 5. Short sprints protect attention and make big tasks feel manageable.

🌱

Growth Mindset

“I can’t do this yet.” Viewing mistakes as feedback helps students persist through challenges and improve over time.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who can participate in EduSpeed?

EduSpeed is intended for students in grades 6–12. Enrollment requirements may vary by school or district.

Do students need to attend every day?

Students may participate according to the schedule established by their school and program coordinator.

Is EduSpeed only for students who need extra help?

No. The program supports students who want academic assistance as well as students who want more opportunities to explore subjects.

What should students bring?

School materials, assignments, and any supplies required for scheduled activities.
