Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who can participate in EduSpeed?

EduSpeed is intended for students in grades 6–12. Enrollment requirements may vary by school or district.

Do students need to attend every day?

Students may participate according to the schedule established by their school and program coordinator.

Is EduSpeed only for students who need extra help?

No. The program supports students who want academic assistance as well as students who want additional opportunities to explore subjects and develop skills.

What should students bring?

Students should generally bring their school materials, assignments, and any supplies required for their scheduled activities.
